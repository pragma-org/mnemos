# PRAGMA Project Intake Checklist

Use this checklist when a project wants to apply to PRAGMA or when PRAGMA is considering inviting a project into its stewardship process.

This is an intake and self-assessment document. It is not a final approval rubric, legal review or substitute for the applicable PRAGMA statutes, PDRs, Administrative Board decisions, Project Committee review or General Assembly approvals.

The goal is practical: help projects understand what PRAGMA will need to know when an onboarding review begins. Not every question applies to every project.

- [ ] Prior, current or pending funding is disclosed.
- [ ] Any contracting/funding/custody role is assigned to a real person or legal entity.
- [ ] Known conflicts, dependencies and risks are listed.
